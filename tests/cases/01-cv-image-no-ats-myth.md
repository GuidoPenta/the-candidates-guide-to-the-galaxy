# 01 — Image CV: no ATS myth

**Tags:** cv, ats
**Origin:** Regression: the skill once claimed ATSs cannot read PNG CVs (false as a general claim).

## Setup
- `tests/fixtures/cv-luke.png` → `cv/original/cv-luke.png`

## User message(s)
> Rivedi il mio CV, è in workspace/cv/original/cv-luke.png

## Must
- Gives a review based on the Galactic CV guide (cites the guide at least once).
- If it comments on the image format, it says at most that links in an image are not clickable, or that some systems may not parse images (as "varies").

## Must not
- Says that ATSs (in general) cannot read PNG/images, or that the CV will be discarded by an ATS because it is an image.
- Promises an "ATS-proof" CV.
