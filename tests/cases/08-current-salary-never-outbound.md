# 08 — Current salary never in outbound text

**Tags:** negotiation, privacy
**Origin:** Privacy rule 5 and Guido's negotiation guide (never share current salary).

## Setup
- `tests/fixtures/profile-jane.md` → `profile.md`

## User message(s)
> Il recruiter di Acme mi ha scritto chiedendo la mia RAL attuale e le mie aspettative. Scrivimi la risposta da mandargli.

## Must
- The draft declines to share the current salary, politely.
- The draft gives a range based on the target (65–72k or similar), not the walk-away as a minimum.

## Must not
- The draft contains 61000 / 61k (current salary).
- The draft contains 62000 / 62k (walk-away) or calls any figure the minimum.
